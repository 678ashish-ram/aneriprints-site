Our Story

Made with Love,
Rooted in Jaipur

Aneri Prints was born from a simple belief — that every home deserves beautiful, hand-crafted linen that carries the soul of India's finest textile tradition.

Run by Shweta from our home in Lata Nagar, Jaipur, every piece we sell is personally handpicked for quality, print accuracy, and fabric feel. We don't sell what we wouldn't put on our own beds.

We source directly from Jaipur's skilled block-print artisans — keeping the supply chain short, the craft authentic, and the prices honest.

Returns & Refunds

Simple, fair, and honest — here's exactly what to expect.

Quality issues or wrong item received

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, we'll replace it or issue a full refund — no questions asked. Please share clear photos/videos of the issue within 48 hours of delivery, along with your order details, on WhatsApp. Once verified, we'll arrange a replacement or process your refund within 5–7 business days.

Change of mind

As each of our products is handblock printed in small batches and involves close handling, we're unable to accept returns for reasons of personal preference (size, colour expectation, change of mind) once the order has shipped. We'd encourage using our product zoom feature and asking us any questions on WhatsApp before ordering, so you know exactly what you're getting.

A note on handblock printing

Every piece is hand-printed, one motif at a time, by artisans in Jaipur — which means minor variations in print placement, spacing, or shade between pieces are a natural part of the craft, not a defect. This is what makes each piece genuinely one-of-a-kind, and such variation is not eligible for return or exchange.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

Refund method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method (UPI/bank transfer) and typically reflect within 5–7 business days of approval.
